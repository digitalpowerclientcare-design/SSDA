Before you call

What to have ready for your astrology or Vastu consultation

  • ✓ Date, time and place of birth. For Kundli matching, both people's.
  • ✓ Your questions, written down, so the time goes on them.
  • ✓ Any earlier chart or reading you have.
  • ✓ For Vastu: the floor plan, and the compass direction the main door faces.
  • ✓ Your preferred language, and whether you will visit or join online.

Two ways

Visit in person or consult online

Both work. Coming in suits a family that wants to sit together and talk with the elders in the room, and online suits you if the drive is the problem or one of you lives in another city.
