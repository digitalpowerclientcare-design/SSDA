How to reach the centre in S.R. Nagar

Look for Andhra Bank first. We are beside it, in Ramakrishna Nivas at Bapu Nagar, Sanjeeva Reddy Nagar. Royal College is directly opposite, which makes a handy second landmark when you stop to ask someone on the road.

We are in a flat and not a street-level shop: Flat No. 502 on the 5th floor, in House No. 247/3RT. Search for Sri Sai Durga Astrology Centre on Google Maps, and the saved pin will take you to the building, though it is worth phoning if you are not sure which entrance to use.

Before you set out

  • Call or WhatsApp +91 81257 03372 so a time is fixed and Pandit Ji expects you.
  • Carry your date, time and place of birth, any earlier chart, and the questions you want covered.
  • Parking and public transport are not listed here yet, so ask when you call.

No. You can consult by phone or video from wherever you are in Hyderabad, or outside it, and Pandit Ji prepares the chart in advance from the birth details you share.
