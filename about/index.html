Reading the chart

Jyotish (Jyothisham): how Pandit Ji reads a Vedic birth chart, step by step

A birth chart is a map of where the planets stood at the moment you were born. Reading it follows an order, and this is the order.

  1. 01

    Lagna, the rising sign

    Everything starts with the sign that was rising on the eastern horizon when you were born. It decides which house is which, and a birth time that is off by a couple of hours can change it.

  2. 02

    The nine grahas in your chart

    Next come the planets, Rahu and Ketu included: which house each one sits in, which sign, and how strong it is there.

  3. 03

    Dasha periods and timing

    Pandit Ji reads the dasha running now against the chart. That is the part that speaks to timing. Without it, a chart only tells you what is possible.

  4. 04

    Kundli matching: both charts together

    For a marriage match the two charts go side by side. The 36-point Guna score is one part of that reading.

  5. 05

    Dosha checks come first

    If a dosha shows up, Pandit Ji first looks at whether any of its cancellation conditions apply.

You will see the same craft spelled Jyotish, Jyotishi, Jyothisham or Jyothishyam, depending on who is writing. It is one tradition. If a word in a reading is new to you, the astrology glossary covers most of them, and how a consultation works walks through a session from the first call.

Where Pandit Ji trained, and which texts he works from, is something to ask him directly. We would rather you hear it in his words than read our summary of it.

Questions about Pandit Ji, his Jyotish and Vastu work, and booking

What does Pandit Ji help with?

Kundli matching, marriage timing and delay, career and business questions, Muhurtham (auspicious dates), doshas, numerology, and Vastu for homes, flats, offices and plots.

Is a pandit the same as an astrologer?

Not always. Pandit is a title of respect for a learned person, and many pandits are priests. A jyotishi (astrologer) is the one who reads charts. Pandit Ji is addressed as Pandit, and the work you come to him for is Jyotish and Vastu.

Will I be pushed into remedies or purchases?

No. If your chart suggests a remedy, Pandit Ji tells you what it is and why, and you can decline. If it suggests nothing, he says that.

Can Pandit Ji guarantee a result?

No, and he will not try. A chart can show which periods look supportive and which look slow. It cannot name a day or promise an outcome. Be careful with anyone who does.

Which languages are consultations held in?

Telugu, Hindi, Kannada and English. Tell us which one you want when you call, and the session is booked in that language.

Can I consult Pandit Ji online from outside Hyderabad?

Yes. Sessions run over phone or video as well as in person, so a relative in another city or country can join the call. Call or WhatsApp to fix a time.
