Vastu

Vastu consultant services for homes, flats, offices and plots

Vastu Shastra is the old science of direction and layout in buildings, and a Vastu consultation looks at the entrance, kitchen, bedrooms, puja room and workspace, then tells you what to move and what to leave alone.

You cannot turn a flat to face another way, but you can move the bed, the stove and the almirah, and in most flats that is where the useful changes are. Nobody needs to break a wall for it.

Facing has its own pages too: west facing, south facing, east facing and north facing house Vastu.
