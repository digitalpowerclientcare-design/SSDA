What a Vastu consultant in Hyderabad looks at

A Vastu consultant reads your floor plan against the eight directions and the five elements, then tells you what to move. Pandit Sri Santosh Sharma Ji works from the plan and the direction the main door faces, or from a visit to the property. He begins with what you can shift yourself, the bed, the stove, the shrine, the heavy almirah, and talks about structural change last.

Hyderabad has a lot of apartment living, plenty of independent houses in the older colonies, and steady building on plots, so the same handful of questions keeps coming round. They are below. We are in Sanjeeva Reddy Nagar, close to Ameerpet, Panjagutta and Somajiguda, and the whole review can be done online if the property is across town. Directions are on the Sanjeeva Reddy Nagar page.

Main door and entrance Vastu

Which way the property faces and where along that wall the door sits. Then what you see when the door opens, and whether a pillar, a pole or a pile of shoes is in the way.

Kitchen, fire and water

Where the stove, sink, water storage and borewell sit. The texts pair the kitchen with fire and give water a zone of its own, and they like the two kept apart.

Bedroom Vastu and sleeping direction

Which room suits the head of the household, where beds and heavy wardrobes go, and which way your head points when you sleep.

Puja room

A clean, quiet spot for the family shrine, and the direction you face while praying. There is a separate guide to puja room Vastu.

Toilets, stairs and storage

Where toilets, store rooms and overhead tanks sit in the plan, and the quiet fixes when one sits badly. Stairs have their own guide on staircase Vastu.

Plot, shape and surroundings

Plot shape, slope, road side and the buildings around it, and planning the layout before the first brick goes up.

Vastu, Vaastu, Vasthu

Vastu Shastra explained: one subject, three spellings

Vastu Shastra is the old Indian system for placing rooms and doors by direction, light, air and the five elements. You will see it written Vastu, Vaastu and Vasthu. It is the same subject. It is also not a single rulebook: the texts and the practitioners differ in detail, so read everything below as the way the tradition is taught and not as law. One awkward room rarely matters on its own, and Pandit Ji weighs the whole plan together.

Vastu directions: what each of the eight is linked with

North
Kubera's side, the lord of wealth, and linked with water. Keep it open: entrance, living room, cash and valuables.
North-east
The most sacred corner, linked with water and a clear head. Puja, prayer, study. Keep it light.
East
The rising sun and Indra, linked with health and new beginnings. Main entrance, living room, windows for the morning light.
South-east
Agni, the fire corner. Kitchen, stove and electrical equipment belong here.
South
Yama, linked with rest and weight. Bedrooms, heavier furniture, storage.
South-west
Earth and Nirrti, the stable corner. Master bedroom, the owner's seat, heavy storage.
West
Varuna, linked with gains and completion. Dining area, the children's study, a second living space.
North-west
Vayu, the air corner, linked with movement and change. Guest room, garage, toilets, stored grain and supplies.

The five elements

Vastu lays the five elements, the pancha bhutas, over the plan of a building. Earth sits in the south-west, water in the north-east, fire in the south-east, air in the north-west, and space in the middle. Most placement advice comes from keeping each zone true to its element. Kitchen in the fire corner, water away from it, the heavy things in the earth corner.

The Vastu Purusha mandala and the brahmasthan

In the classical picture a site is laid over a square grid of 64 or 81 squares. A figure called the Vastu Purusha lies across it, head to the north-east and feet to the south-west, and each part of the grid has a presiding deity and a direction.

The middle of the grid is the brahmasthan. It is kept open and light, with no heavy structure, toilet or staircase on it. You cannot open up the middle of a modern flat, so the working advice there is shorter: keep that central zone clear and clean.

The same picture explains the north-east and south-west. One is light and calm, the other heavy and settled. That is why so much Vastu advice puts the puja room in the north-east and the master bedroom in the south-west.

Looking for the best Vastu consultant? What to ask before you book

Ask what the consultant will work from. A floor plan with the main door marked is a fair answer. A verdict given before anyone has seen the plan is not.

You may have been told: "Fixing Vastu means breaking walls." In a modern apartment the walls are mostly shared or structural anyway, so the useful changes move a bed, a stove, a shrine or a cupboard. If a structural change would help, Pandit Ji tells you, and puts a cheaper option next to it.

"A flat that faces the wrong way should be avoided." No facing is bad by itself. The facing is fixed and the inside of the flat is not, which is why the flat Vastu page spends its time on the inside.

"Put a crystal or a pyramid in the corner and it is sorted." Layout comes first. A small object is at best a supporting detail.

"A Vastu defect means misfortune is certain." Vastu flags a placement as something to look at and says nothing certain about your future. It doesn't promise money, health or success either, because it sits next to effort, finances and looking after yourself, and none of those can be skipped.

Moving into a new house or opening an office? The date is a separate question. See Griha Pravesh muhurtham in Hyderabad and Muhurtham.

Vastu consultation questions people ask before they book

How much does a Vastu consultation cost in Hyderabad?

Fees are shared before booking. Call or WhatsApp with the property type (home, flat, office, shop or plot) and what you want reviewed, and we will tell you the fee before you commit to anything.

Is a site visit necessary, or can a floor plan be reviewed?

A floor plan with the direction of the main door marked is enough to begin, and it works well for flats and for properties you are still thinking of buying. A visit shows more of the surroundings and the actual rooms, so ask for one where those matter, but it is not the only way.

Will I have to break walls or rebuild anything?

Not as a first suggestion. Most concerns are fixed by moving things: the stove, the bed, the shrine or the heavy storage. If a structural change would help, Pandit Ji says so and puts a cheaper option beside it.

Can a property be checked before I buy it?

Yes, and it is one of the best times to ask. Send the floor plan and the facing before you pay a booking amount, and you will see which layout points are easy to live with and which would need a compromise.

Does Vastu guarantee wealth, health or success?

No. Vastu Shastra is a guide to planning and arranging a space well. It sits beside effort, finances and health care, and no outcome is promised.

Is Vaastu or Vasthu different from Vastu?

No. Vastu, Vaastu and Vasthu are three spellings of the same subject, Vastu Shastra. You will see all three on signboards and in searches, and they mean the same thing here.
