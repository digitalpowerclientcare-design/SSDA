Vastu for a house that faces east

East Facing House Vastu: A Good Facing Still Needs a Good Plan

Say you are about to buy an east facing house because everyone agrees east is the best side. They have a point. Here is what the rest of the plan has to do to deserve it.

Written by Pandit Sri Santosh Sharma Ji · Vedic Astrologer & Vastu Consultant · Last updated 4 October 2026 · How we write

Why people ask for an east facing house

The east is where the sun comes up, and Vastu Shastra treats it as the direction of light, health and new beginnings. A house with its door on the east takes the first sun into the front rooms.

That is a real comfort, whatever you believe about Vastu. Morning light in the front room and the puja corner is pleasant in any city, and in a Hyderabad summer it comes before the heat does.

Now the caution. Vastu Shastra traditionally rates the east highly, yet practitioners differ on the finer points, and a facing alone does not make a house sound.

What the facing cannot do

It can't move a toilet out of the north-east, clear a staircase from the middle of the house, or fix a kitchen that opens onto the front door. Those are decided inside the plan, and they are what a review looks at.

East facing house Vastu: where the door sits on the wall

Many practitioners prefer the door in the north-east half of the east wall. The south-east end is the one they like least. That's the usual advice, and some schools split the wall into nine parts and argue over each, so treat any single number you hear with caution.

Take the compass reading at the door, not from the road, and keep the phone away from metal and gates. Then mark the door on the plan.

If the door falls at the south-east end, nobody is asking you to sell. Look at what stands in front of it, whether it opens freely, and how the passage inside runs. A clear, well lit entrance helps in any facing.

Rooms in an east facing house

Three rooms decide most of a Vastu review of an east facing plan.

Kitchen in an east facing house

South-east is the first choice, north-west the second. In an east facing house the south-east is a front corner, so ask where the smoke and the smell go, and whether the stove can be seen from the door. The cook faces east. Keep the sink away from the burners.

Master bedroom and children's rooms

The south-west corner is the heavy one, and in this plan it is at the back, quiet and well away from the road. That suits a master bedroom. The head goes towards the south or east. Children's rooms go west or north-west, and the study table is turned east or north.

Puja room in an east facing house

The north-east is the old puja corner, and here it is a front corner that gets the first light. That is a gift. Keep it clear of a toilet wall and out from under a staircase. For idols and what to do in a flat, see the puja room guide.

Where an east facing plan goes wrong

Three slips we'd look for first. None of them is rare, and none needs a hammer.

A toilet in the north-east. The texts discourage it more than almost any other placement, and in an east facing house it sits at the front. Whether it can be softened depends on the plan, and the first thing to ask is whether a different bathroom can carry the daily use.

A staircase in the middle of the house. The middle of the house, the brahmasthan, is meant to stay open. If the stair is already built, keep the space around it clear and read the staircase Vastu guide before you do anything.

Everything heavy in the north-east. Water and light belong there. A loaded store room, a stack of old furniture or a big almirah do not. Move the weight to the south and west, which costs you an afternoon.

Pandit Ji starts with moves like these. Changing the structure of the house is the last thing on the list.

East facing plot or flat

Building from scratch? The plot Vastu page covers shape and road direction. Buying a flat? A flat's facing is fixed, so see Vastu for flats and apartments for what you can change inside.

Griha Pravesh in an east facing house

The date is a Panchang matter, handled through Griha Pravesh muhurtham. The layout is a separate matter, and it is easiest to adjust before the furniture moves in.

East facing home: questions people ask

Is an east facing house good as per Vastu?

Vastu Shastra rates the east highly because of the rising sun and the morning light. But the facing is only the front of the house. A kitchen in the wrong corner or a toilet in the north-east can spoil a good facing, so the rooms behind the door still need checking.

Which is better, an east facing or a north facing house?

The old texts speak well of both, and neither beats a well planned house of the other. Choose between two houses by their plans, light and position, and weigh the compass last. If you are comparing the two, our north facing page sets out what to check.

Where should the main door be in an east facing house?

Many practitioners prefer the north-east half of the east wall and avoid the south-east end. Take a compass reading at the door and mark where it falls on the plan, because schools differ on the exact stretch.

Where should the kitchen be in an east facing house?

South-east is the first choice and north-west the second. In an east facing house the south-east is a front corner, so check where the stove stands, which way the cook faces and whether the kitchen opens straight onto the entrance.

Do I need a Vastu check if my house already faces east?

It is worth one look at the plan. A good facing does not fix a toilet in the north-east or a staircase in the middle of the house. If the plan is clean, Pandit Ji will say so, and you can ask the fee before you book.

Is an east facing flat good for Vastu?

It gets morning light, which is a real advantage, and its facing is fixed. What you can change is inside: the bed, the stove, the shrine. The apartment page lists the checks for a flat, including balcony and shared walls.
