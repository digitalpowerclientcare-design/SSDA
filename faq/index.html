Kundli and marriage

Kundli matching, horoscope matching and janam kundli questions

What the 36 points mean, what they leave out, and why a marriage can be slow.

What is Kundli matching (horoscope matching), and what does the 36-point score mean?

Kundli matching, also called horoscope matching or Guna Milan, compares two birth charts before a marriage is fixed. The Ashtakoot system awards up to 36 points across eight factors, such as temperament, health and family. The score is where the reading starts. The charts and any doshas decide the rest.

Read the full Guna Milan guide →

How many Gunas are needed for marriage? Is 18 out of 36 enough?

Eighteen is the usual cut-off. But neither 18 nor 28 settles anything alone. Say a match scores 24: it clears the cut-off, and a serious dosha can still matter. A lower score can sometimes be balanced by strengths elsewhere in the two charts, which is why the reading goes past the number.

Kundli matching in Hyderabad →

Is Kundli matching enough to decide a marriage?

No. The score uses the Moon sign and birth star, which is a narrow slice of a chart. Pandit Ji also looks at the seventh house, any doshas and the running dasha of both people. After that, the two families still have to talk about work, money, where to live and what each side expects.

How Kundli matching is done →

What is a Janam Kundli, and what does a birth chart reading cover?

Janam Kundli is your birth chart, drawn from the date, time and place you were born. A reading goes through the Lagna, the nine grahas and the running dasha, then turns to what you came to ask: marriage, work, money, a decision you are stuck on. The exact birth time matters here.

Janam Kundli reading →

Why is my marriage getting delayed, and can astrology explain it?

Astrology looks at the seventh house, its ruler and the dasha you are in. That can show why things feel slow and which periods look easier. It cannot give you a date. And not every delay sits in the chart. How the search is run, and what the family expects, count too.

Doshas

Mangal dosha, Nadi dosha and Kaal Sarp dosha questions

What each one means, and when a remedy is worth thinking about.

What is Mangal dosha (Manglik), and does it make a match impossible?

Mangal dosha means Mars sits in certain houses of the chart. It does not rule out a match. The texts give several conditions under which it is cancelled or reduced, for example when both partners carry it. An app's one-line flag cannot tell you whether any of those apply, so get both charts read.

Dosha remedies →

What is Nadi dosha, and how serious is it in Kundli matching?

Nadi carries the most points in the Ashtakoot score, 8 of the 36, and the dosha shows when both partners fall in the same Nadi. That weight is why people worry about it. It has cancellation conditions too, so the flag alone is not the end of a match. Pandit Ji checks those first.

Nadi dosha explained →

What is Kaal Sarp dosha?

The old texts say it forms when all the planets in a chart sit between Rahu and Ketu. How much it matters depends on the exact placement and on the dasha running now, and that varies a great deal from chart to chart. The label by itself tells you very little.

Kaal Sarp and other doshas →

Do I need a remedy if a dosha shows up in my chart?

Not always. Many doshas are cancelled by something else in the chart, or are weak to begin with, and Pandit Ji looks for that before he says anything about a remedy. If one does make sense, he tells you what it is and why, and the decision is yours. Nobody should be hurried into it.

Muhurtham

Muhurtham and Griha Pravesh questions

Choosing a date for a wedding, a housewarming or an opening.

What is Muhurtham (Muhurat), and when do I need it?

Muhurtham is a date and time chosen for an event, using the Panchang and the charts of the people involved. Families ask for it before a wedding, a Griha Pravesh, a business opening or a naming ceremony. The date is picked for your event and your people.

Muhurtham service →

How do I find a Griha Pravesh muhurtham in Hyderabad?

Tell us about the house, who will live in it and the weeks you are considering. Pandit Ji checks the Panchang and the family charts and suggests dates that work. If the Vastu is still a question, send the floor plan as well, so the date and the layout get settled together.

Griha Pravesh muhurtham →

How early should I ask for a Muhurtham date?

As early as you can. Halls, priests and relatives all have to be arranged around the date, and that is easier to do with weeks in hand than with days. Tell us the event, the people involved and any dates that cannot work for the family, and Pandit Ji suggests options from what is left.

Numerology

Numerology questions

Birth numbers, name spellings and how they sit next to a chart.

What is numerology, and how is it different from astrology?

Numerology works from numbers. Your birth number and destiny number come from your date of birth, and a name carries values of its own. Astrology works from where the planets stood in your birth chart. Here, numerology sits next to the chart and does not replace it.

Numerology →

Should I change my name spelling because of numerology?

Not automatically. A spelling change touches your official records, so weigh the paperwork as well. Name analysis gives you a second angle on a question. It is not a fix on its own, and whether any change is worth the trouble is your call.

Vastu

Vastu consultant questions: flats, houses and plots

What to move, what to leave, and when to ask before you buy.

Is Vaastu the same as Vastu?

Yes. Vastu, Vaastu and Vasthu are different spellings of Vastu Shastra, the old Indian system for placing rooms, doors and furniture in a building. However you spell it when you search, you are asking for the same thing.

Vastu and other terms →

Do Vastu corrections mean breaking walls?

Usually not. You cannot turn a flat to face another way, but you can move the bed, the stove and the almirah, and in a flat that is where the changes tend to be. If a structural change ever would help, Pandit Ji says so and gives you a cheaper way to do it as well.

Vastu consultation in Hyderabad →

Can a Vastu consultant check my flat from the floor plan?

Yes. Send the plan with the main door's compass direction marked. A floor-plan review works for a flat you live in or one you are thinking of buying, and a visit shows more, but you do not need one to make a start.

Vastu for flats and apartments →

Does Vastu work for a rented flat in Hyderabad?

Within limits, yes. A tenant cannot move a kitchen, but can move a bed, a desk, a puja shelf or an almirah. The facing and the walls stay as they are. Pandit Ji tells you which changes are worth making in your flat and which you can leave alone.

Flat Vastu →

Is a south-facing house bad for Vastu?

No, but it deserves a closer look. The bad name comes from the old link between south and Yama. What the texts care about is where along the wall the door sits and how the rooms are laid out inside. Send the plan before you decide either way.

South-facing house Vastu →

Is a west-facing house good or bad as per Vastu?

Neither, on its own. A west-facing house has its own points to plan around, such as heat on the west wall in the afternoon and where the door sits. A well-planned one is a good home. East, north and south-facing houses get the same treatment: check the layout first.
