Kundli Matching (Guna Milan) Explained: The 36 Points

Kundli matching compares two birth charts across eight factors worth 36 points in all. This guide goes through each factor, shows how to read the score, and explains why a number should never decide a marriage on its own.

Written by Pandit Sri Santosh Sharma Ji · Vedic Astrologer & Vastu Consultant · Last updated 4 October 2026 · How we write

What kundli matching and Guna Milan check

Kundli matching, also called Guna Milan or horoscope matching for marriage, compares two birth charts using the Ashtakoot system. The system scores eight factors, each worth a fixed number of points, up to a maximum of 36. Every factor comes from the Moon sign and birth star (nakshatra) of the two partners.

Nobody passes or fails. The point is to look at temperament, mental understanding, emotional bond, health and family welfare, and to flag the spots that deserve a longer conversation before the families go further. Most add a check for doshas such as Mangal dosha, and once a wedding is being planned, a Muhurtham for the date.

This page explains the method. To have your own two charts matched, see kundli matching in Hyderabad. If you only have one chart and want it explained, that is a janam kundli reading.

The eight Ashtakoot factors

Here they are in the usual order. Points climb from 1 to 8, so the later factors carry more weight.

Factor Points What it looks at In everyday words
Varna 1 Temperament and ego The old texts grade outlook and attitude to work. Today people take it to mean how the two temperaments sit together, and it ranks nobody’s background.
Vashya 2 Mutual influence How easily the two adapt to each other, and whether one of them tends to dominate.
Tara 3 Birth star harmony How the two birth stars (nakshatras) relate to each other. The texts link it to well-being and shared fortune.
Yoni 4 Physical and instinctive fit Each birth star carries a symbolic animal. The match looks at natural affinity and intimacy between the pair.
Graha Maitri 5 Mental compatibility Whether the lords of the two Moon signs are friends. Read as how well the two understand and support each other.
Gana 6 Nature and temperament Birth stars fall into three types: Deva, Manushya and Rakshasa. The labels are old. What the match checks is how alike or different the two natures are.
Bhakoot 7 Emotional bond and family welfare How the two Moon signs sit against each other. Linked to emotional closeness and the welfare of the household.
Nadi 8 Health and constitution Birth stars fall into three Nadis. The same Nadi for both partners gets flagged, and Nadi carries the most points of all.
Total 36 Sum of all eight factors

Nadi dosha and Bhakoot dosha: the two heavy factors

Nadi (8) and Bhakoot (7) carry the most points, so a zero on either one pulls the total down hard, and that is why a reading looks at them on their own and not only at the sum. For the Nadi side in full, see Nadi dosha.

How to read a Guna Milan score

These are the ranges most often quoted. Use them to open the conversation. They do not close it.

Score out of 36 How the texts read it
Below 18 Read as weak. Look closely at the charts before going ahead.
18 to 24 Read as acceptable. Families often proceed from here once the other factors have been checked.
25 to 32 Read as good compatibility.
33 to 36 Read as very good.

Is 18 out of 36 enough?

Say your daughter’s match comes back with 24 points. That clears the usual cut-off of 18, so on paper it looks fine. But 24 does not cancel a serious dosha, and a score of 16 can sometimes be balanced by what the charts show elsewhere in the two lives. Treat 18 as a reason to keep reading the charts, and drop the idea of a pass mark.

Horoscope matching for marriage: why the score alone does not decide

A Guna Milan score comes from just two things per person, the Moon sign and the birth star, while a full chart holds far more, so a proper reading adds four more things before it says anything about a match.

  • How strong each chart is on its own. A high score between two weak charts says less than a modest score between two well-supported ones.
  • The doshas: Mangal dosha, and the Nadi and Bhakoot findings, each checked against its cancellation conditions.
  • Dasha timing. The planetary periods running for each partner affect when a marriage is likely to go well.
  • The human side: values, health, money, family expectations and how the two people feel about each other. None of that can be scored.

Pandit Ji reads both charts together, and he checks any dosha against its cancellation conditions before he treats it as serious, because a flag on its own proves very little. No chart can promise how a marriage will go. He does not pretend otherwise. Medical, legal and money questions go to the right professionals.

Nadi dosha and Mangal dosha: when the texts cancel them

Can Nadi dosha be cancelled?

Sometimes. If two people share a birth star but have different Moon signs, or share a Moon sign but have different birth stars, several sources treat the dosha as cancelled, although sources differ, so the actual charts decide. The full walk-through is on the Nadi dosha page.

Mangal dosha and its cancellation conditions

Mangal (Kuja) dosha arises, say the texts, when Mars sits in the first, fourth, seventh, eighth or twelfth house of the birth chart, and in some schools the second as well. You may have heard the word first from an app. “The app says Manglik. Does that mean we drop the match?” Not on that alone.

The texts list several conditions that reduce or cancel it. Which of them apply depends on the actual chart. These are the ones quoted most:

  • Both partners carry Mangal dosha, which the texts say balances it.
  • Mars sits in its own sign or its sign of exaltation.
  • Mars is joined or aspected by a benefic planet such as Jupiter.
  • Counted from the Lagna, the Moon and Venus, the dosha does not show strongly from all three.

If the dosha is there and nothing cancels it, Pandit Ji may suggest a simple remedy and tell you the reason for it. Dosha remedies covers how he handles that.

Questions

Kundli matching questions people ask

What is a good Guna Milan score?

18 out of 36 is the usual minimum for an acceptable match, scores from 25 to 32 count as good, and anything above 32 as very good. The score is only part of the reading, because the full charts, any dosha and the timing matter just as much.

Is kundli matching necessary for a love marriage?

That is for the couple and the families to decide. Some couples who chose each other still want the charts read, or want a good Muhurtham for the wedding. Treat the result as something to talk over. The couple’s own decision comes first.

Is online kundli matching accurate?

An app does the arithmetic correctly when it has the right birth details, and the arithmetic is all it does. It gives you the 36-point score and often a dosha flag. A flag only tells you a rule fired. It cannot tell you whether a cancellation condition applies to your two charts.

What if the score is below 18?

That does not end the discussion. Look at which factors lost the points. A zero in Nadi or Bhakoot weighs differently from small losses spread across the other six, and the rest of both charts can balance some of it. Send both charts and ask.

Can matching be done if the birth time is not known?

With care. The Moon sign and birth star drive the 36-point score, and they depend on an accurate chart, so a time near a nakshatra boundary can change the result. Say so when you book and Pandit Ji will tell you which findings are firm and which are approximate.

Can kundli matching be done online?

Yes. Matching works from the birth details of both people, so it can be done by phone or video as well as in person at Sanjeeva Reddy Nagar. Send the date, time and place of birth for both partners when you book.
