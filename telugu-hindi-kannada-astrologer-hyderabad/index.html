Why it matters

A Telugu astrologer in Hyderabad you can speak to in Telugu

A chart reading is a conversation. You describe the worry, the astrologer asks about dates and family, and then explains what the chart shows. That goes better when nobody is translating in their head.

In many families the person who most needs to hear the answer is a parent who speaks Telugu, Hindi or Kannada at home. So pick the language when you book, and bring them along, in person or on the call.

Pandit Sri Santosh Sharma Ji has practised in Hyderabad for more than 35 years. The method does not change with the language. A Mangal dosha check, a kundli match or a Vastu review is done the same way in all four.

Questions

Consulting in Telugu, Hindi or Kannada

Can I consult a Telugu astrologer in Hyderabad in Telugu?

Yes. Pandit Ji consults in Telugu, Hindi, Kannada, English. Say which you prefer when you call or message, and the whole conversation follows that language.

Do you consult in Hindi and Kannada as well?

Yes. Hindi and Kannada consultations are held the same way as Telugu ones, in person at the centre or by phone or video. Tell us your language when you book.

Does the language change the reading?

No. The chart and the method are the same. What changes is how comfortable you and your family are asking questions and following the answer, which matters most when parents are joining the call.

Can I send my details in English letters and speak in Telugu?

Yes. Most people message their birth details in English letters and then talk in the language they prefer. You do not need to write anything in Telugu, Hindi or Kannada script.
